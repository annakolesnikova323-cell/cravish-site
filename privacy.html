Privacy policy

Cravish for iPhone · Last updated: 26 November 2026

What stays on your device

Everything you enter in the app — your tastes, diets, allergens and reminder time — is stored only on your device, and so are your list of saved dishes and your shopping list. We have no access to them.

Anonymous usage statistics

Cravish sends anonymous usage statistics through Amplitude (Amplitude, Inc., USA). These are events such as “recipe opened” or “cooking finished” — including which recipe, as a recipe code — together with the app version, iOS version, device model, language and a random device identifier.

The statistics also say when the subscription offer was shown, which plan was picked, and whether a purchase or a restore went through, was cancelled or failed. They never include payment details.

We do not send your allergens, diets or other restrictions, or anything you type. We do not collect your name, email address, IP address or location, and we do not use advertising identifiers.

On phones set to a region in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, Brazil or South Korea the statistics are off unless you turn them on, and you can switch them on or off at any time: Profile → Settings → About Cravish → “Send anonymous statistics”. Elsewhere they are on; if you would rather we didn’t keep them, write to us and we will delete what was collected from your device.

Measuring our own ads

We advertise Cravish on Facebook and Instagram. To learn which ads bring people to the app, Cravish includes the Meta SDK (Meta Platforms, Inc., USA, and Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd.). Apple treats this as tracking, so the app asks first, once, with Apple’s prompt “Allow Cravish to track your activity across other companies’ apps and websites?”.

Only if you tap “Allow”, the app tells Meta that it was installed and each time it is opened, with your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA), the app version, iOS version, device model, language and time zone; Meta’s servers also see your IP address. Meta links this with the ads it showed you, to tell us which ads worked. Nothing about what you cook or save, no allergens or diets and nothing you type is ever sent. Meta handles this data under the Meta Privacy Policy.

If you tap “Ask App Not to Track”, the Meta SDK is never started and sends nothing. Apple may still tell Meta that an install came from one of its ads, without any information about you (SKAdNetwork). You can change your answer at any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

The app doesn’t ask while usage statistics are off, or on phones set to a region in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, Brazil or South Korea.

Subscription

Cravish Premium is a subscription sold through the App Store. Apple takes the payment: we never see your card, your Apple Account or your name. The app only asks the App Store whether a subscription is active, to open the app, and keeps that answer on your device.

You can cancel or change the subscription at any time in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Subscriptions are sold under Apple’s standard licence agreement.

What we don’t do

We don’t sell data, we don’t show ads in the app, and we don’t track you across other apps or websites unless you allow it (see “Measuring our own ads”). You don’t need an account to use the app.

Notifications

The dinner reminder and the timer alert are local notifications created by your device itself. You can turn them off in the app or in iOS Settings.

Deleting your data

To delete the data on your device, delete the app. To ask us to delete anonymous statistics, write to us. If you allowed tracking, what the Meta SDK sent is kept by Meta under its own policy; we can’t see or delete it per person.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page.
