Privacy policy

Cravish for iPhone · Last updated: 16 November 2026

What stays on your device

Everything you enter in the app — your tastes, diets, allergens, saved dishes, shopping list and reminder time — is stored only on your device. We have no access to it.

Anonymous usage statistics

If you agreed to it when you first opened the app, Cravish sends anonymous usage statistics through Amplitude (Amplitude, Inc., USA). These are events such as “recipe opened” or “cooking finished”, together with the app version, iOS version, device model, language and a random device identifier.

We do not send your allergens, diets or other restrictions. We do not collect your name, email address, IP address or location, and we do not use advertising identifiers.

Nothing is sent without your consent. You can turn the statistics off or on at any time: Profile → Settings → About Cravish → “Send anonymous statistics”.

What we don’t do

We don’t sell data, we don’t show ads, and we don’t track you across other apps or websites. You don’t need an account to use the app.

Notifications

The dinner reminder and the timer alert are local notifications created by your device itself. You can turn them off in the app or in iOS Settings.

Deleting your data

To delete the data on your device, delete the app. To ask us to delete anonymous statistics, write to us.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page.
