Cravish for iPhone

cravish.support@gmail.com

How do I change my allergens or diet?

Open Profile, tap the gear, and pick what applies under Allergens and Diet. A tap applies straight away. Dishes with your allergens are not hidden — the allergen is flagged on the recipe and on the ingredient. Always check the packaging too, especially for a serious allergy.

How do I turn the dinner reminder on or off?

Profile → gear → Dinner reminder → “Remind me every day”, then choose the hour. If the switch turns itself back off, notifications for Cravish are off in iPhone Settings → Notifications → Cravish.

How do I take something off the shopping list?

In Cart, hold a product and choose “Remove from list”. To take off a whole dish with its products, tap “For … dishes” above the list and use the bin next to the dish.

Where are my saved recipes?

In Profile. Tap the heart on a card to take a recipe off the list.

How does Cravish Premium work?

Cravish Premium unlocks the whole app. New subscribers get a free week; after it, the plan you chose — yearly or monthly — starts and renews automatically until you cancel. The app shows the price and the date of the first payment before you confirm, and Apple takes the payment.

How do I cancel the subscription?

iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Cravish → Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the free week or the current period ends, and you won’t be charged for the next one. Cravish stays open until the end of the period you’ve paid for.

I have a new phone or reinstalled the app

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open Cravish and tap “Restore” at the top right of the offer screen. Your subscription is found again.

How do I ask for a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple: go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple Account and choose Cravish.

How do I delete my data?

Everything is stored on your iPhone, so deleting the app deletes it. See the privacy policy for details.

Reporting a problem

Tell us what you did and what happened, and include the version shown at the bottom of Profile → Settings (for example “Version 1.0 (2)”). For a recipe, its name is enough.
